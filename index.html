Everything you need to know to play like a pro

The Court

Glass Walls (Ball can bounce off)
Wire Mesh
Net (88cm center, 92cm sides)

Scoring

🎯Game!
Deuce (40-40): Must win by 2 clear points
Advantage: Next point after deuce - win it to take the game
Tiebreak at 6-6: First to 7 points (win by 2)

Serving

1 Serve underarm, hitting ball at or below waist height
2 Stand behind service line, bounce ball then hit
3 Ball must land in opposite diagonal service box
4 Two serves allowed (like tennis) - fault on missing both
5 Let: replay if ball hits net and lands in correct box

Walls & Bounces

✅
Valid Returns
  • Ball bounces on floor, then off back wall
  • Ball bounces on floor, then off side wall
  • Hit ball before or after it hits wall
  • Ball can go out of court via door/gap after bouncing
❌
Faults / Lost Points
  • Ball bounces twice on floor
  • Ball hits wall before bouncing on floor (on serve)
  • Ball hits wire fence before crossing net
  • Ball hits you or your partner

Pro Tips

🎯
Use the Walls

Don't be afraid to let the ball hit the back wall - you often get an easier shot

🤝
Communication is Key

Call "mine" or "yours" to avoid confusion with your partner

📍
Control the Net

The team at the net has the advantage - work your way forward

🎾
Placement Over Power

Accuracy wins points - don't just smash everything

Equipment

🏓
Padel Racket
Solid face with holes, no strings. Max 45.5cm long.
🎾
Padel Ball
Similar to tennis ball but slightly less pressure.
👟
Court Shoes
Non-marking soles with good grip for quick movements.
